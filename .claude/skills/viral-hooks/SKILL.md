# Viral Hooks

A library of proven hook frameworks. The first 3 words of a post decide whether it gets read, so this is the single highest-leverage sentence in any post. Called by `post-writer` to open every post, and by `repurpose` to open every repurposed piece. Can also be called standalone any time a draft has a flat opening line.

## How to use this library

1. Read the idea/topic and (if available) `brand-brief.md` for voice and the contrarian belief.
2. Pick 2-3 frameworks below that best fit the topic and platform.
3. Write 2-3 candidate hooks, each under 12 words, each a complete standalone claim (not a setup — a hook, not a preamble).
4. Prefer specific numbers, named details, and concrete stakes over vague claims.
5. Return the strongest one, plus the runner-up in case the caller wants an alternative.

## Hook framework categories

### Receipts (proof-based)
- "I tested [N] [things]. Here's what actually worked."
- "[N] [things] later, here's the only one that mattered."
- "I tracked every [metric] for [timeframe]. The result surprised me."
- "Screenshots don't lie: [specific result]."
- "Here's the exact [process] that got [specific result]."
- "I spent $[N] finding out [thing]. Save your money."
- "[N] days. [N] [units]. Here's what happened."
- "I asked [N] [people] the same question. The answers split hard."
- "The data on [topic] says the opposite of what everyone claims."
- "I checked. [Common belief] is wrong."

### Reverse (contrarian)
- "Most people think [common belief]. They're wrong."
- "Everyone tells you to [common advice]. Do the opposite."
- "[Common belief] is costing you [specific cost]."
- "Stop [common practice]. It's not working."
- "The advice to '[common advice]' is bad advice."
- "You don't need [assumed requirement] to [desired outcome]."
- "[Popular tactic] is dead. Here's what replaced it."
- "The best [role/thing] I know breaks this 'rule' constantly."
- "Unpopular opinion: [contrarian belief]."
- "If you believe [common belief], you're leaving [thing] on the table."

### Stolen lessons (borrowed authority)
- "I copied [specific person/brand]'s [tactic]. Here's what happened."
- "I stole this from [source] and it worked better than my own idea."
- "A [role] once told me [lesson]. I ignored it for years. Mistake."
- "The best [thing] advice I ever got came from [unexpected source]."
- "I reverse-engineered how [admired example] does [thing]."
- "[Person]'s worst [thing] taught me more than their best one."

### Curiosity gap
- "The one thing nobody tells you about [topic]."
- "There's a reason [surprising fact], and it's not what you think."
- "Here's what happens right before [outcome]."
- "The part of [process] nobody shows you."
- "What I noticed after [N] [units] of [activity]."

### Confession / vulnerability
- "I got [outcome] wrong for [timeframe]. Here's what fixed it."
- "The mistake that cost me [specific cost]."
- "I almost quit [thing] because of this."
- "Nobody warned me [thing] before I started."

### Direct value / list
- "[N] ways to [desired outcome] without [common obstacle]."
- "[N] things I wish I knew before [milestone]."
- "The [N]-step process for [outcome]."

### Warning / stakes
- "If you're doing [common practice], stop."
- "This mistake is quietly killing your [metric]."
- "Nobody tells you [thing] until it's too late."

### Comparison
- "[Option A] vs [Option B]: I tried both for [timeframe]."
- "The difference between [good outcome] and [bad outcome] is one habit."

## Customizing

This file is meant to be edited. Add hooks proven to work in the user's own niche directly to the relevant category above, or create a new category if none fits. Prune ones that consistently underperform.
