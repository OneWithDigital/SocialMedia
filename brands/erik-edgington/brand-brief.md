> Captured: 2026-07-01
> Update this file as the business evolves.

## Business
Personal brand, not tied to one employer. Erik wears two hats — founder of One With Digital (agentic AI agency) and a credit union manager — and the personal brand is about the *skills* to be successful using AI/tech in business and finance, drawing on both experiences without being locked to either.

## Customer
Broad: other business owners, agency operators, and finance/banking people interested in practical AI/tech use — with an added focus on building local connections and business relationships (this is as much about networking/community as it is about audience size).

## Primary CTA
Follow for more — build the audience first, rather than selling directly.

## Strong Opinion / Wedge
The traditional agency/business model is dying — the shift is toward agentic AI doing the work that used to require a big team. Same core wedge as One With Digital, but told through Erik's personal experience and voice rather than the company's.

## Story Vault
- Built agentic AI agents to handle social media, blog, and SEO content — personal proof point for the "AI does what used to take a team" wedge.

## Voice
Expert and authoritative + warm and personal — confident on the tech/finance substance, but personable and relationship-first (fits the local-connection goal).

## Universal Voice Rules (apply to every post)
- Contractions always ("don't" not "do not")
- Active voice, short sentences
- Address the reader as "you"
- Numbers as digits ("3 tips" not "three tips")
- No em dashes — use commas, periods, or "..."
- One concrete idea per post, not three
- Specific details over generic statements
