# Brand Brief — John's Sanitation

> Captured: 2026-07-01
> Update this file as the business evolves.

## Business
Septic tank pumping, porta potty rental, restroom trailer rental, and handwashing stations. Based in South Lyon, MI, serving Southeast/Midwest Michigan (Ann Arbor, Northville, Brighton, and surrounding communities). Fleet of 2,000+ units with GPS-tracked trucks.

## Customer
Three main types depending on the service: homeowners needing reliable septic pumping, event planners needing porta potties/restroom trailers for one-off events, and contractors/construction site managers needing job-site toilets and handwashing stations.

## Primary CTA
Call or request a quote.

## Strong Opinion / Wedge
Most companies in this industry treat customers like a number — slow to respond, hard to reach, inconsistent service. John's Sanitation actually shows up, answers the phone, and tracks trucks by GPS so customers know service is reliable. Reference this in "most sanitation companies do X, we do Y" and contrarian-take posts.
