## Business
Full-service social media and AI content operations for small businesses: content strategy, writing, design (Canva), and scheduling (Blotato) — increasingly built on AI/agentic workflows rather than a traditional agency team/headcount model.

## Customer
Owner-operators of local small businesses (no in-house marketing team) who are too busy running the business to post consistently and don't trust generic agency reports.

## Primary CTA
Book a call / inquire.

## Strong Opinion / Wedge
The traditional agency model is dying. We're moving in an agentic AI agency direction — replacing bloated account teams and vanity-metric reports with AI-driven systems that actually produce and ship content. Reference this in "most agencies do X, we do Y" and contrarian-take posts.

## Voice
Expert and authoritative + scrappy and underdog — confident and ahead of the curve, but calls out the old way agencies operate.

## Universal Voice Rules (apply to every post)
- Contractions always ("don't" not "do not")
- Active voice, short sentences
- Address the reader as "you"
- Numbers as digits ("3 tips" not "three tips")
- No em dashes — use commas, periods, or "..."
- One concrete idea per post, not three
- Specific details over generic statements
